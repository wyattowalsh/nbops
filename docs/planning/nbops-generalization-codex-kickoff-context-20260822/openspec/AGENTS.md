# OpenSpec guidance for `nbops`

- Accepted behavior belongs under `openspec/specs/`.
- Proposed work belongs under `openspec/changes/`.
- `build-colab-observer` is the stable historical Google Colab-first baseline change ID.
- `generalize-notebook-runtime-observer` is the active change that adopts `nbops` and introduces platform-neutral runtime profiles and adapters.
- Keep `spec.md` observable and behavior-level.
- Put packages, source paths, commands, sequencing, migration mechanics, and agent instructions in design, tasks, planning docs, or handoffs.
- Reconcile downstream tasks, validation, traceability, diagrams, and handoffs after proposal/spec/design changes.
- Do not apply, sync, verify, or archive without complete implementation evidence and explicit approval.
